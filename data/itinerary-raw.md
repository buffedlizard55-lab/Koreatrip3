## Week 1 South Korea Trip - Myeongdong

### Day 1: Sunday - Travel from SFO to Seoul. (Oct.31/Nov 1)
1. Flight from SFO to ICN Airport, South Korea
2. Travel from ICN to Seoul
   a. Arex (airport train) 45 min, $7
   b. Airport Limosine bus $13
3. Check into Stanford Hotel in Myeongdong
   a. Light neighborhood exploration, settle in, and rest from jet lag.

### Day 2: Monday - Namsun Tower & Myeongdong. (Nov 2,2006)
1. Take bus to Namsun Tower (N Seoul Tower). 31 min. bus ride. Cost:
   ~For cable car + Observatory ticket: $31 each.
   -Hours: Observatory 10:00-10:30 (entry until 10:00)
   -Cable Car Hours: 10:00-10:30 (weather permitting)
2. Myeongdong Shopping District (Seoul's main shopping district)
   -Fashion, food stalls, k beauty.
   Note: Department Store Hours: Lotte/Shinsegae 10:30-8:00pm (weekdays), 10:30-8:30pm (weekends)
   Cosmetics Stores: Olive Young 8:00-12:00am, Major brands 10:00-10:00pm
   Street Food: Most stalls 11:00-10:00pm, some open until 11:00pm
3. Night Market Exploration. -Myeongdong Night Food Market & Gwangjang Market-Authentic
   local cuisine. Netflix famous-get hand-cut noodles. Hours: 9:00-11:00pm.
   (Some food stalls open until 2:00a.m.)
   Or go to Mangwon Market -less touristy
   Dinner: Eat at Pig Restaurant 2 - Premium Quality -Itaewon
   Or Dinner: Myeongdong Kyoja. Michelin hand-made dumplings. There may be a line.
   Crystal Jade -korean/chinese food (2 locations in Myeongdong. Go to the Shinsegae
   Dept Store location -basement floor. Or Hongojib (full bbq for 1 for only $10)
   Bars: Myeongdong Sookhee

### Day 3: Tuesday - Gyeongbokgung Palace + Bukchon Village (Nov. 3)
1. Gyeongbokgung Palace (Hours: November-February 9:00-5:00pm (Last admission 4:00.
   Closed: Tuesdays (If Tuesday falls on a public holiday, closed on following business day))
2. Must-see: Main palace of Joseon Dynasty, changing of the guard ceremony
   (10:00 AM, 2:00 PM, closed on Tuesdays)
   Time needed: 3-4 hours for full visit
   Admission: 3,000 KRW (~$2.30), Free for under 19 and over 65
3. Lunch at traditional restaurant near palace
4. Bukchon Hanok Village (traditional Korean houses). Highlight: Authentic Korean
   architecture, photo opportunities, craft shops
   -Cafe Onion Anguk, in Hanok (Arrive before it opens, there will be a line)
5. Traditional tea house experience.
   -Activity: Korean tea ceremony, learn about tea culture
   -Operating Hours: Typically 12:00-9:00, some open until 10:00

### Day 4: Wednesday - Seong-Su (the Brooklyn of Seoul) (Nov. 4)

   fashion pop-ups, & artisan bakeries
3. Hanjungsun-different fruit mochis.
4. Caricature- they draw you as an animated character
5. Eternalog -fancy photo booth experience.
6. Shopping
   -Check out: Blue Elephant- eyewear store like Gentle Monster
   -Cafes: Cafe Far Ben -pistacio latte with cream top. Cafe Onion. Rain Report Cafe.
   -Jayeondo Sogeumppang -salt bread
   -Musina Standard- basics store

   -Ader Error -contemporary streetwear
   -Brandy Melville-cute clothes
6. Break Silence -R&B & cocktails
7. Dinner- Eat at Chuncheongol Dakgalbi (unlimited lettuce and perilla wraps)
   -Uri Wang Galbitang located in Gangdong. Favorite of Ikseon-dong
