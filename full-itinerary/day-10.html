Tuesday · Cheongsapo (Haeundae-gu)		 Cheongsapo Daritdol Observatory (Over-Water Glass Skywalk) Draft: "6. If you don’t book round trip capsule train tickets, walk the Cheongsapo Daritdol Observatory -sea-facing skywalk in Haeundae District. Take pictures."
Verified via VisitKorea: 72.5 m-long, 20 m-high over-water skywalk with transparent floor, free entry, open 09:00–21:00 in Sep–Nov.  167 Cheongsapo-ro, Haeundae-gu, Busan Mar–May & Sep–Nov: 09:00–21:00 | Dec–Feb: 09:00–20:00 | Jun–Aug: 09:00–22:00 Open year-round (restricted in severe wind/rain) Free (shoe covers provided at entrance) From Cheongsapo Blueline Park Station, walk ~5–8 min (400 m) east along the coastal boardwalk to Cheongsapo Daritdol Observatory.
