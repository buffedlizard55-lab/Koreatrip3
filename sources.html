How to read this guide

Proposed plans come from your itinerary. Sourced facts come only from official operator, government or tourism pages checked in this session. “Partly sourced” does not verify the whole row. Unknown hours, closure days, addresses and prices stay empty. Context-only links are explicitly labeled and do not confirm named businesses.

Sources checked 26 October 2026, not a travel-year assumption. Search extracts are labeled below; they are not represented as full-page reads. Exact operational conditions, reservations and performance cancellations require live confirmation. No currency conversion, invented transit duration or date-specific availability is supplied.
