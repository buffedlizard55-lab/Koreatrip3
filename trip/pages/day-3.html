Day 3 — Gyeongbokgung Palace + Bukchon Hanok Village

🚨 This day does not work as written: Gyeongbokgung Palace is closed every Tuesday, and the Royal Guard Changing Ceremony does not run on Tuesdays. Tue Nov 3, 2026 is not a Korean public holiday, so the holiday exception does not apply. Suggested fix: swap Day 3 with Day 4 (Wed) or Day 6 (Fri). Bukchon + Cafe Onion still work on Tuesday — see the Flagged for Review page.

🗒️ The plan (in the itinerary's own order)

  1. Gyeongbokgung Palace — main Joseon palace; Royal Guard Changing Ceremony (10:00 / 14:00); allow 3–4 hours (itinerary estimate).
  2. Lunch at a traditional restaurant near the palace (no venue named).
  3. Bukchon Hanok Village — traditional houses, photo spots, craft shops. Cafe Onion Anguk (arrive before opening — expect a line).
  4. Traditional tea house / tea ceremony experience (no venue named).

🚇 Getting there (from the hotel)

  • Hotel → Gyeongbokgung: Line 2 Euljiro 1-ga → Euljiro 3-ga, transfer Line 3 → Gyeongbokgung Station. Gwanghwamun Station (Line 5) also serves the palace's main gate.
  • Palace → Bukchon / Cafe Onion: Walk east toward Anguk Station (Line 3). Cafe Onion Anguk is ~3 min from Anguk Exit 3 (official store page).
  • Bukchon timing rule: Tourist sightseeing in the Red Zone (Bukchon-ro 11-gil) is allowed 10:00–17:00 only — be out of the residential alleys by 17:00.
