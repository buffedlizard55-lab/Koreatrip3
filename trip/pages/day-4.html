Day 4 — Seongsu — “the Brooklyn of Seoul” (+ DDP)

📅 Wednesday, Nov 4, 2026  ·  📍 Seongsu-dong · Dongdaemun (DDP)  ·  0 verified 1 partial 0 conflict 16 unverified

⚠️ Geography check: the Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP) is in Dongdaemun, not Seongsu — and the itinerary's description (“industrial warehouses converted into flagship fashion pop-ups & artisan bakeries”) actually describes Seongsu-dong's café street. Both are on Subway Line 2, so combining them is easy, but budget the extra hop. Also: most Day 4 indie shops/cafés could not be confirmed from official sources — their hours are left blank and flagged rather than guessed.

🗒️ The plan (in the itinerary's own order)

  1. Solsot (Seongdong-gu) — stone-pot rice with sides; “come before opening time.”
  2. Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP).
  3. Hanjungsun — fruit mochi · Caricature portrait · Eternalog photo booth.
  4. Shopping/cafés: Blue Elephant (eyewear) · Cafe Far Ben (pistachio latte) · Cafe Onion · Rain Report Cafe · Jayeondo Sogeumppang (salt bread) · Musina Standard · Marge Sherwood · Ader Error · Brandy Melville.
  5. Break Silence — R&B & cocktails.
  6. Dinner: Chuncheongol Dakgalbi, or Uri Wang Galbitang.

🚇 Getting there (from the hotel)

  • Hotel → Seongsu: Line 2 direct: Euljiro 1-ga → Seongsu Station (same line as the hotel — no transfer). Seongsu café street clusters around Yeonmujang-gil, a short walk from the station.
  • Seongsu ↔ DDP: Line 2: Seongsu → Wangsimni → Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station; DDP connects directly to that station (official DDP page).
