Day 1 — Arrive Seoul — SFO → Incheon → Myeongdong

📅 Sunday, Nov 1, 2026  ·  📍 Incheon Airport → Myeongdong, Seoul  ·  3 verified 1 partial 0 conflict 0 unverified

🗒️ The plan (in the itinerary's own order)

  1. Fly SFO → ICN (Incheon International Airport). Depart Oct 31, arrive Nov 1 (as written in itinerary; exact flight/times not in the plan — see Flags).
  2. Transfer ICN → Seoul: AREX airport train or airport limousine bus.
  3. Check in at Stanford Hotel Myeongdong; light neighborhood walk, settle in, rest from jet lag.

🚇 Getting there (from the hotel)

  • Fastest to Seoul Station: AREX Express (non-stop) from ICN T1/T2 → Seoul Station, then Subway Line 4 one stop to Hoehyeon/Myeongdong area or a short taxi to the hotel. AREX does not go to Myeongdong directly.
  • Easiest with luggage: Airport Limousine Bus 6015 (or 6001) from ICN → Myeongdong stops near the hotel. Buy the ticket at the airport counter/kiosk before boarding.
  • Cheapest: AREX All-stop train to Seoul Station, then Line 4. Slower, commuter-style seating.
