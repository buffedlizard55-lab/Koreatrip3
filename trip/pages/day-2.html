Day 2 — N Seoul Tower & Myeongdong

📅 Monday, Nov 2, 2026  ·  📍 Namsan · Myeongdong · Gwangjang Market (evening)  ·  4 verified 4 partial 2 conflict 5 unverified

🗒️ The plan (in the itinerary's own order)

  1. Bus up to N Seoul Tower (Namsan); observatory visit.
  2. Myeongdong shopping district — fashion, K-beauty, food stalls (Lotte / Shinsegae department stores, Olive Young).
  3. Evening: Myeongdong night food market + Gwangjang Market (Netflix-famous hand-cut noodles), or Mangwon Market (less touristy alternative).
  4. Dinner options in itinerary: Pig Restaurant 2 (Itaewon) · Myeongdong Kyoja · Crystal Jade (Shinsegae B1) · Hongojib. Bar: Myeongdong Sookhee.

🚇 Getting there (from the hotel)

  • Hotel → N Seoul Tower (bus): Subway Line 4 Myeongdong → Chungmuro (1 stop), Exit 2, then Namsan Sunhwan Shuttle Bus 01A / 01B to N Seoul Tower (₩1,500, transport card only). Bus 01A also stops at “Myeongdong Cathedral · Namdaemun Tax Office,” walkable from the hotel.
  • Hotel → N Seoul Tower (cable car): Walk or short taxi to the Namsan Cable Car lower station near Myeongdong, ride up (₩15,000 round trip), short walk to the tower.
  • Myeongdong → Gwangjang Market: Subway Line 2 Euljiro 1-ga → Euljiro 4-ga, or Line 1 to Jongno 5-ga. Market is at 88 Changgyeonggung-ro, Jongno-gu.
  • Myeongdong → Mangwon Market: Line 2 to Hapjeong, transfer Line 6 to Mangwon Station, Exit 2 (≈350 m walk).
