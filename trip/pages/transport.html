🚇 Transport — getting everywhere on this plan

Base 1: Stanford Hotel Myeongdong (Seoul) · Base 2: L7 Haeundae (Busan). All fares/times below are official unless a row says otherwise.

💳 One card for everything: pick up a transport card (e.g., T-money) at the airport — the Namsan shuttle bus is card-only (₩1,500), and subway/bus transfers are simplest by card. The airport limousine bus needs a ticket bought at the airport counter/kiosk before boarding.
