Day 9 — Seoul → Busan (KTX) — Haeundae

📅 Monday, Nov 9, 2026  ·  📍 Myeongdong → Seoul Station → Busan → Haeundae  ·  3 verified 2 partial 0 conflict 0 unverified

🗒️ The plan (in the itinerary's own order)

  1. Check out of Stanford Hotel by 12:00; aim to leave by 10:00.
  2. Uber/taxi to Seoul Station. Be at the KTX ~45 min early to secure luggage space (itinerary's own buffer).
  3. KTX to Busan (itinerary estimates 2 h 45 min — varies by train, see table).
  4. Uber/taxi Busan Station → L7 Haeundae by LOTTE HOTELS. Check in & relax.
  5. Optional walk toward Haeundae Beach; evening street food at Haeundae Market (hotteok, tteokbokki, mandu, Korean fried chicken, seafood).

🚇 Getting there (from the hotel)

  • Hotel → Seoul Station: Taxi (itinerary's choice) or Line 4 Myeongdong → Seoul Station (2 stops via Hoehyeon). With luggage, the taxi is the low-stress option.
  • Seoul Station → Busan Station: KTX high-speed train. Book on Korail's official site/app — tickets open ~1 month ahead; pick the train by its stated journey time (stops vary).
  • Busan Station → Haeundae (subway option): Line 1 Busan Station → Seomyeon, transfer Line 2 → Haeundae Station; hotel and market are in the Haeundae Station/beach area. Itinerary chooses taxi — fine with bags.
