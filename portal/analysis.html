Pacing & Logistics Analysis Across Days 1–9

Key structural findings from auditing the draft itinerary

1. Calendar Year = 2026 (9/9 Weekday Match)

Every single weekday/date pair in the draft (Sun Nov 1 through Mon Nov 9) aligns exclusively with 2026. Day 2's Nov 2,2006 is a 1-digit typo (Nov 2, 2006 was a Thursday).

2. Severe Front-Loading vs Empty Weekend

Day 2 (14 entries) and Day 4 (16 entries) pack 58% of the entire trip into two days while Day 7 (Itaewon) and Day 8 (Sunday) have zero scheduled venues. Moving Itaewon BBQ + Mangwon Market + surplus cafes to Days 7–8 makes every day relaxed.

3. Green Line 2 Advantage

Stanford Hotel Myeongdong sits 2 minutes from Euljiro 1(il)-ga (202), putting Gwangjang (204), DDP (205), Seongsu (211), and COEX (219) on the exact same subway line with zero transfers.
